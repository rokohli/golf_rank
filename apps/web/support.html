Frequently Asked Questions

How do course rankings work?

Fairway uses pairwise head-to-head comparisons to build your personal course leaderboard. Whenever you rate a course you’ve played, the app asks you to compare it against other courses on your list. These ratings also contribute to the overall 1–10 community rating displayed for each course.

How do I log a round?

From any course detail page or the Rounds tab in the Fairway mobile app, tap Log round. You can record your total score, tee boxes, playing date, hole notes, and attach photos taken during your round.

How do green fee estimates work?

When an official green fee is missing from our catalog, community members can submit standard 18-hole walking or riding rates. Once multiple agreeing suggestions are submitted, the fee estimate is automatically verified and displayed for all golfers.

What do the budget tiers ($, $$, $$$, $$$$) mean?

Budget tiers help tailor course discovery and trip recommendations to your preferred spending range:

  • $ (Budget / Muni): Up to $50 per round
  • $$ (Standard Public): Up to $100 per round
  • $$$ (Premium Public): Up to $175 per round
  • $$$$ (Splurge / Destination): $250+ per round

You can also adjust your exact maximum green fee anytime under Profile > Golf preferences.

How does a photo become the course cover photo?

When you attach photos to a public round, your best shots are considered for the course's featured cover photo! Fairway selects cover photos based on image resolution, landscape composition (16:9 ratio), and clarity. If your photo is selected, it will be showcased at the top of the course page and across search results for all golfers.

How do I export or delete my data?

You have complete ownership of your data. You can download an instant JSON export of your profile, ratings, and rounds via Settings > Download my data in the app. To permanently delete your account, authentication credentials, and all associated round history, select Delete account at the bottom of Settings.
